\section*{Data availability}
All software, extracted case-study data, experiment configurations, the full per-scenario simulation records, and the validation records supporting this paper are openly available in the \texttt{fresh-daugherty} repository at \url{https://github.com/UBC-FRESH/fresh-daugherty}. The curated supplement---case-study data and validation anchors (\path{supplementary/01-case-study-data.md}), core-grid results (\path{supplementary/02-core-grid.md}), objective-gap-diagnostic evidence (\path{supplementary/03-gap-diagnostic.md}), per-landbase detail (\path{supplementary/04-landbases.md}), the extension analyses (\path{supplementary/05}--\texttt{08}), and the replanning-institution and robustness analyses (\path{supplementary/10-review-analyses.md}), with figures and tables---starts at \url{https://github.com/UBC-FRESH/fresh-daugherty/tree/main/supplementary/README.md}. The complete benchmark record---the scenario summary and the per-scenario projected and realized harvest trajectories for all 432 scenarios, together with the exact regeneration command and pinned dependency versions---is tracked under \path{results/experiments/} (\url{https://github.com/UBC-FRESH/fresh-daugherty/tree/main/results/experiments}) and archived with a DOI at \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23218533} (release v0.2.0). The Umpqua planning documents are public-domain US Forest Service works accessible via HathiTrust (records 002547999 and 002439528).
