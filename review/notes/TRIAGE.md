## How a round works

1. Export the comments in Overleaf (browser extension, or `review/bin/oce-export.sh`
   if you decide to export on this box — see `review/README.md`).
2. Transfer the export folder here.
3. `python3 review/bin/review_import.py <export folder> --label round-2`
4. Read `review/current/comments.md`; work through the generated table below and
   fill in the last column.
5. Reply to and resolve threads in Overleaf after you have addressed them.
